Best Practices

Solo Traveler Safety Protocols

📍 Live Location Check-ins

Always share your real-time WhatsApp or Google Maps location with a family member or buddy before starting remote treks.

📑 Document Redundancy

Keep offline digital backups of your Passport, Government IDs, and Travel Insurance policy on your phone and Google Drive.

💵 Split Emergency Cash

Never store all money in one wallet. Stash backup currency and cards in your main backpack liner or jacket inner pocket.

🏨 Verified Accommodations

Choose hostels and homestays with verified reviews, 24/7 front desks, and strong female traveler security ratings.
